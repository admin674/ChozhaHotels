About US

Enjoy a Luxury Experience

CHOZHA HOTELS, has carved a niche for itself in the hospitality industry with its commendable service and delectable facilities, since its inception in 1973.

We redefine comfort with our state-of-the-art architecture to facilitate optimum usage of natural light and air and a warm ambience. Customer satisfaction being our main focus, our relentless efforts towards consistent quality, and constant upgradation, provide us the impetus to deliver the best..

Our founder chairman, Mr. M. K. Sundaram is actively associated with both national and international bodies like International Federation of Asian and Western Pacific Contractor's Association (IFAWPCA). He is in constant pursuit of knowledge through which the business benefits. He was actively involved in the Committee on Affordable housing and the Committee for the bifurcation of the Chennai Metropolitan area into three districts. He has also presented papers in CMDA meetings, attended national and international seminars, training programmes and conferences..

Chozha Hotels shares a special bond with all its customers inspired by our Founder Chairman’s spirit of celebrating constant congenial relationship with all his associates. He stays in touch with the public by sharing his thoughts with them through both print and the visual media, periodically..
