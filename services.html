Temple Timings

Situated in the temple town of Thirukadaiyur, famous for its various shanthi homams (worship through fire), Chozha Hotels offers services, to facilitate such worship, to its customers.

Thirukadaiyur temple pooja timings is morning 06:00 to 12:30. and Evening 04:00 to 08:30
